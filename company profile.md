## Company overview

Al-Rawasi is a Libyan engineering consultancy headquartered in Al-Bayda, with published branch locations in Benghazi and Tripoli. Its services span planning and studies, architectural and engineering design, project management, technical supervision, safety consulting, real estate development, urban planning, testing supervision, and training.

The company presents its approach as a combination of local understanding, international engineering practices, technical precision, economic efficiency, and architectural quality. Its portfolio emphasizes religious buildings, banking and administrative facilities, commercial developments, and customer-facing spaces. The director's statement also emphasizes preserving Libya's religious and historical identity. [S3–S8]

## Vision, mission, and working philosophy

- **Vision:** Raise the quality, safety, prevention, and service standards of public and private engineering projects through distinctive design and advanced execution supervision.
- **Mission:** Apply modern technology and international expertise to deliver precise, innovative engineering services with quality, economic efficiency, and architectural excellence.
- **Design philosophy:** Balance function, aesthetics, and sustainability when shaping living and working environments.
- **Working environment:** Connect people, management responsibilities, policies, methods, and technology; encourage shared leadership and participation in decisions; develop staff capability and team relationships.
- **Technical approach:** The projects page states that the company uses Building Information Modeling (BIM) to improve precision and reduce wasted time and resources. No software names or detailed BIM deliverables are supplied. [S3–S8]
